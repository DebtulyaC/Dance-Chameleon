How To Play

Get In Position

  1. 01Allow camera access and frame your upper body — head down to your hips — in the shot. Legs don't matter for this game.
  2. 02Each level shows a glowing target pose. Study the arm positions before the countdown ends.
  3. 03When the timer starts, strike the pose and hold it. A live match meter shows how close your arms are.
  4. 04Hit 80% similarity or higher, held steady for a moment, to clear the level — the faster, the better.
  5. 05Clear all levels to lock in your total time and average accuracy, then save your run to a leaderboard file on your computer.
